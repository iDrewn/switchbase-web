Switchbase Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Switchbase is a personal collection-tracking app for press-on nails. It is an independent, unofficial project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Switch Nails.

This page explains what data the app collects, why, and what control you have over it.

What stays on your device

Everything in your collection — photos, and the Art. no, Model, Shape, and EAN fields for each item — is stored locally on your device only. None of it leaves your device unless you explicitly turn on collection sharing (see below).

What the Community feature collects

The Community tab lets you optionally share your collection with other users of the app. This requires no sign-up:

  • An anonymous device identity. The app uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to assign your device a random identifier. This is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal information.
  • A username. A random username is generated automatically the first time you open the Community tab. You can change it to anything you like; please don't use your real name if you'd rather stay anonymous.
  • Sharing is off by default. Nothing about your collection is sent anywhere until you turn sharing on yourself, from your profile in the Community tab.
  • What gets shared, if you turn it on: only the Model, Shape, and Art. no of each item in your collection. Photos and EAN codes are never shared or uploaded, regardless of your sharing setting.
  • Turning sharing off deletes your shared items from our servers immediately.

Shared data is stored using Firebase (Google Cloud) and is only used to power the Community tab's browsing feature within the app. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising — the app shows no ads and includes no advertising or analytics SDKs.

What we don't collect

  • No account, email address, or phone number is ever requested.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising identifiers or analytics/tracking SDKs.
  • No photos are ever uploaded anywhere — they stay on your device.

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data (your collection, photos, and settings).

Because the Community feature uses anonymous accounts rather than a sign-up system, there's no traditional account to "close." Turning sharing off deletes your shared collection items right away. If you'd like your username/profile record removed from our servers entirely, contact us at the address below and we'll delete it.

Third-party services

The Community feature is built on Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), a Google Cloud service. Data in transit is encrypted (TLS). Firebase's own privacy practices are described at the link above.
